No generic stock animals. This page uses real photographs documented in Wilpattu National Park.

01 · Panthera pardus kotiya

Sri Lankan Leopard

A flagship Wilpattu sighting and one of Sri Lanka's most iconic predators.

02 · Melursus ursinus

Sloth Bear

Elusive and special to encounter in the dry-zone forest.

03 · Elephas maximus

Elephant

Often encountered around open areas and water sources.

04 · Water buffalo

Water Buffalo

A common sight in the park's open landscapes and wet areas.

05 · Chital deer

Spotted Deer

Look for herds grazing and moving through the forest edge.

06 · Mugger crocodile

Mugger Crocodile

A reptile associated with freshwater lakes, marshes and slow-moving water.
